# MIKEYS POKER CLUB design sources

The identity uses vermilion, ink, and warm paper; condensed display type; original screenprinted silhouettes; and physical card-table materials. Game rules and saved preference keys are retained.

## Artwork

`public/wild-hand-posters.png` is a four-panel poster atlas used by the menu as a CSS sprite. Actual size: 1774 × 887 pixels.

Panels 1 (Hold'em) and 2 (Blackjack) are character-free vector posters generated by `design/posters/build.mjs`: a royal-flush fan over a spade sunburst, and an ace and jack slammed over a halftone sun. The script writes `holdem.svg` and `blackjack.svg` beside itself and composites them over `design/posters/atlas-base.png`, which holds panels 3 and 4. Rebuild with `node design/posters/build.mjs`.

Panels 3 (Solitaire) and 4 (Roulette) were generated with OpenAI image generation from this prompt; its panel 1 and 2 descriptions are superseded by the vector posters:

> Create an original premium game-menu illustration atlas, landscape 2048x1024, divided into FOUR EQUAL VERTICAL PANELS with no gutters, each a separate complete composition, same consistent screenprinted art direction. No text, no letters, no typography, no logos, no watermarks. Style: sophisticated pop-punk / 1970s British punk gig poster, Andy Warhol two-tone silkscreen, bold editorial noir silhouettes, constructivist poster diagonals, stylish Japanese game key-art energy. Original characters and imagery only. Palette strictly near-black ink #131313, warm ivory paper #f3edda, vermilion red #e83235; coarse selective halftone and subtle ink misregistration, bold solid silhouettes, rich negative space, minimal tiny detail. Panel 1: close-up sharply dressed human card shark in profile, angular tousled hair, holding a fan of playing cards near the face, cool confident silhouette, black dominant on red. Panel 2: elegant female casino dealer with short angular bob haircut and tailored jacket, holding two playing cards, ivory dominant on black, dramatic foreshortened hand. Panel 3: striking elegant gloved hand arranging a cascade of playing cards like a paper sculpture, red/black silhouette against ivory, graphic fan of cards. Panel 4: a close-up roulette wheel seen at dramatic angle with a hand launching a small white ball, black and ivory wheel against red, strong concentric graphic geometry. Frame subjects large, upper 80 percent of each panel, allow lower 20 percent to remain simple for labels added by code. NOT a screenshot or UI mockup. Beautiful finished illustration, class and attitude, not childish, not photorealistic, no gradients, no recognizable existing game characters.

## Fonts

Anton and Barlow are self-hosted under `public/fonts/`. Both use the SIL Open Font License; their license files are included alongside the font files.

- [Anton source](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/anton)
- [Barlow source](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/barlow)

The interface uses no externally hosted font requests.
